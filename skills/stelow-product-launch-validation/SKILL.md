# Launch Validation: From Offer to Commitment

**Objective**: leave the idea field and test whether people will demonstrate genuine interest and real commitment — with their time or their money.

---

## Part 1: Develop and Evaluate the Offer

### What to Do
- Develop launch promotions, build trust, and create bonuses.
- Experiment with different promoters, starting with yourself.
- Experiment with a combination of channels and marketing messages.
- Structure the search for early adopters with a **Pioneer Program**.

### Pioneer Program (Co-Creation Instead of Transaction)

Formalize the search for early adopters who want to help build the solution:
- **Central offer**: extended and free access to the solution (e.g., full annual plan).
- **Clear counterparty**: explicit commitment to provide feedback (e.g., 1 feedback call every X months).

The value offered attracts people who feel the pain intensely. Real commitment is validated in the next stage.

### What Risk Are We Mitigating?
Value risk — whether people are interested.

### What to Observe (Signals)
Interest: curiosity to know more, clicks on ads, email responses.

**Experiment with solutions that have high interest.** Example: at least 60% of the last people contacted demonstrated interest? Before giving up, confirm you're reaching the appropriate audience.

> 📖 For promoter types and advertising channels, see `stelow-product-promoters` and `stelow-product-ads`.

## Part 2: Assess Commitment

Go beyond curiosity. Ask people to invest something valuable: **their time or their money**. Separate curious people from true early adopters.

### Pre-Sale Methods

- **Direct pre-sale to small group**: for 10 people you have a connection with — via in-person conversations, phone, or messengers.
- **Pre-sale to email list**: create an email with a pre-sale opportunity.
- **Pre-sale on landing page**: create a landing page with pre-sale functionality. Tools: Notion (public page), Tally, Gumroad, Instapage.
- **Pre-sale at live event or recorded webinar**: present and sell during the event.
- **Lifetime Deals**: generate initial cash flow and user base. Single payment with massive discount. Platforms: AppSumo. ⚠️ High commission and demanding customer profile — evaluate with caution.

### Waitlist Strategies

- **Referral**: ask for email + referral of up to 3 people. Low commitment but reaches new people. Tool: GetWaitlist.
- **Symbolic Investment + Proportional Discount**: ask for email + symbolic payment. Offer proportional discount at launch (e.g., paid R$10, factor 10, gets R$100 discount).
- **Symbolic Investment + Predicted Price + Proportional Discount**: same as above but display the predicted solution price. Validates closer to reality.
- **Discount with Correct Guess — Price Discovery**: ask for email + price guess. If within 10% interval, get 30% off at launch.

### Other Methods

- **Marketplace ad**: advertise and observe sales (Facebook Marketplace, Mercado Livre, OLX, Reddit).
- **Contact after interview**: ask for email and phone right after interviewing. Tell them you'll contact when ready.
- **App**: a mobile app with one basic function to measure downloads and opens.
- **Fake Door**: measure intention without having the product. ⚠️ When someone "knocks", deliver value anyway.
- **Influencer**: offer free access to an influential person in the niche in exchange for sharing with their audience.

### Pioneer Program in Exchange for Feedback

While pre-sale tests willingness to pay, this tests willingness to invest time and attention.

**What to observe**:
- **Adhesion rate**: how many accept the terms (access in exchange for feedback)?
- **Quality of commitment**: do they show up for calls? Is feedback superficial or in-depth?

A pioneer who pays with time and ideas can be as committed as one who pays with money.

### What to Observe (Signals)

When commitment is high enough, you can validate and create a simplified version:
- X pre-sales
- X waitlist subscribers
- X in affiliate commissions
- X podcast listeners on first episode
- Fully funded or X% of a crowdfunding campaign
