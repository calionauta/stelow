Types of micro products to explore:

- Live event or workshop
- Consulting or mentoring
- Niche authority blog
- Affiliate marketing
- Service provision
- Dropshipping

### Product Strategies (Jobs to Be Done — 5 Types)

**Differentiated Strategy**: serve underserved consumers. Offers better performance in accomplishing the job, charging more for it. Offers unique benefits or features not found in other products, meeting specific needs in a differentiated way.
*Examples*: Uber Black, iPhone.

**Dominant Strategy**: serve all types of consumers (underserved and well-served). Offer better performance in accomplishing the job and charge less. Become the best solution in the category for a specific job, outperforming the competition in performance or value.
*Examples*: UberX, Netflix — more convenient and cheaper.

**Sustaining Strategy**: continue serving current customers with incremental improvements in existing products or services to continuously meet the constantly evolving needs of customers. No major differentiations in performance or price.

**Discreet Strategy**: serve consumers with restricted options. Charge more for limited or worse performance. Focus on niches or success criteria of ignored customers, attending to jobs that are not the main focus of large competitors.
*Examples*: food sales and ATM at airports; lending to indebted people.

**Disruptive Strategy**: serve over-served consumers and non-consumers. Charge less and with limited or worse performance. Introduce changes that redefine how a job is accomplished, usually making it cheaper or more accessible.
*Example*: Uber Pool.

## 📚 Inspirations and Method References

The e-book and this method are inspired by the following works and authors:

- **Ash Maurya** — core principle: *"Life is too short to build something that nobody wants."*
- **Noah Kagan** — *Million Dollar Weekend: The Surprisingly Simple Way to Launch a 7-Figure Business in 48 Hours* — validating is finding 3 customers in 48 hours who will pay money for your idea
- **Sahil Lavingia** — *The Minimalist Entrepreneur: How Great Founders Do More with Less* — the valuable manual process as the true MVP
- **Marty Cagan** — article *The Four Big Risks* — never go more than two weeks without putting your ideas in front of real users and customers
- **Alex Hormozi** — *$100M Leads* and *$100M Offers: How to Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No* — MAGIC structure, trust building, guarantees, and bonuses
- **Tony Ulwick / Strategyn** — *Outcome-Driven Innovation (Jobs to Be Done)* — market definition by functional purpose, creativity triggers for business models
- **Justin Wilcox** — FOCUS framework, one of the founders of Teaching Entrepreneurship
- **Alberto Savoia** — *The Right It* — idea validation before building
- **Ryan Robinson** — *9 Steps to Validate a Business Idea in 30 Days with Less than $500* — manual launch process
- **SEDIF Framework** — to find the audience
- **Transtheoretical Model of Change** — basis of the 5 stages of awareness used in communication and ads
- **NFX** — "white-hot center" concept for marketplaces
- **Article "The Intersection of Fit: Market ⋂ Product ⋂ Channel ⋂ Model"** — the four types of compatibility for a viable business
