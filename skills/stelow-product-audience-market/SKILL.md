# Audience and Market: Find, Understand, and Define

**Objective**: explore the problem space — contexts, difficulties, and needs of people actively seeking to solve something. Then translate those learnings into a clear market definition.

---

## Part 1: Find and Understand the Audience

### How to Find People — SEDIF Model

*(S=Follow, E=Entertain, D=Invest, I=Go/Attend, F=Frequent)*

**Follow**: who do they follow or who inspires them? (friend network, work colleagues, authors and influencers)

**Entertain**: how do they have fun? (hobbies, places)

**Invest**: what do they invest money and time in? (conferences, events, stores for resources they use)

**Frequent**: what places do they frequent (physical or virtual) to talk about the subject? (forums, communities — Facebook, Reddit, etc.)

### How to Create a Space to Gather People

- **Webinar** on the topic — ask for name and contact to register.
- **WhatsApp, Telegram group, or Discord server** bringing together people interested in the topic.
- **Quiz** on the topic — ask for name and email to receive results. Tools: Tally, Scoreapp, Limesurvey.

### How to Understand People

Discover:
- What is the person's context when facing the current challenge?
- What is the person's purpose at that moment?
- What are the needs related to that purpose?

**Conduct interviews** as open conversations without pressure or fixed structure — people should feel like they're chatting. Consult *The Mom Test* for how to have this type of conversation.

Other sources: past conversations, interviews, surveys, support tickets.

**Remember**:
- Don't talk about your idea — focus on the problem.
- Talk about past experiences, not the future. Instead of "what would you do if...?", ask "tell me about the last time you needed...".

## Part 2: Define the Market

**Market Definition (Jobs to Be Done)**:
> A **(1) group of people** who want to accomplish a **(2) functional purpose** (action or expected result).

⚠️ Focus on **functional purpose** (action or expected result), not emotional or social purposes. Emotional and social purposes are important for experience and communication, but don't offer practical direction for innovation.

### Examples of Markets with Functional Purposes
- [Music enthusiast] who wants to [listen to music]
- [Person] who wants to [find a new job]

### Segments

Segment = group of people trying to achieve the same purpose, but facing different challenges.

Examples:
- [Music enthusiast] who wants to [listen to music] [while on the street]
- [Unemployed person with long experience] who wants to [find a new job]
- [Person] who wants to [find a new job] [in a new city]

Strategically, focus on 1–3 segments.

### GPN Story (Group, Purpose, Needs)

After defining the market and refining the list of needs:

> As [group or segment] who wants to [purpose] [optional: context], I want a solution that makes it easier, faster, cheaper, and more accurate to: [need 1], [need 2], [need 3]...

**Example**:
> As [unemployed person with long experience] who wants to [find a new job], I want a solution that makes it easier, faster, cheaper, and more accurate to:
> - Identify opportunities for my experience level
> - Demonstrate the value of my experience
> - Prepare for the selection process
